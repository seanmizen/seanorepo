Chart

Tap a bar to start from there, or a section name to jump to it. While playing, the jump lands on the next bar line so you stay in time. The little strip under each bar marks where the chords change.

Edit the song

This is the whole song file. The settings come first: title, artist, slug (the address, made from the title when you leave it out), key, tempo, suggest (up to two transpositions, e.g. -2, -4) and notes.

Write the chords in the original key. [Name] starts a section and | splits bars. Two chords in a bar land on beats 1 and 3. Three land on 1, 2&, 4. Four get one beat each. To place a chord yourself, add @beat, e.g. Bm7 Esus4@3 E@4.

Shortcuts: Space play or stop · ← → previous or next section · 0 original key · 1 2 the suggested keys · F follow the playing bar again.

The chart scrolls along with the music. Scroll it yourself at any time to look ahead or back, and it stops following until you press Back to bar or tap a bar.

The grey numbers under each chord are its number in the key. They don't change when you transpose, and that's how a pianist can switch key on the fly.
