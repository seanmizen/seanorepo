Tube Sounds

Procedural London Underground train sounds. The Web Audio API makes every sound live. There are no samples.

    Train and stock

    A change of stock builds a new train at the same place and speed. It stops a running script. A change of motor keeps the train and the script.

    Scripts

    Full service: the brakes go on at t = 0 s, 100 m out at 20 m/s. The train stops at 9 s, the doors are open from 10 s to 24 s, and the train departs at 27 s. The approach starts 12 s before t = 0 s.
